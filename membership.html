Meet the Team

David Salazar
Head Coach — USATF Certified

I competed in junior high in the long jump, 200 and 400 meters. Didn’t compete again until I was 39. USATF national champion numerous times in the 800, 1500 meters and pentathlon. Ranked as high as number 1 in the world in the 800 and pentathlon. One club 4x800 world record in 2004. Won my first USATF National title in 1992 and my last USATF title in 2012. Won the Canadian Nationals in 1996, 800 meters, and the following week the USATF Nationals in the 1500. Top 3 finishes in most master’s championships that I competed in from 40-75, both indoors and outdoors. Senior Nationals champion 2015 in the 800. Fastest 5k in my early 40s 15.45 My best event has been the 800 and these are my fastest times in each age group.
