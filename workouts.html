Schedule

Weekly Workouts & Events

Sessions vary by time and location depending on weather, season, and workout type. Members receive full details by weekly email.

Tuesday
Running Workout

Running workouts tailored to ability - sprints, middle distance, and long distance workouts run concurrently.

Time: 6 PM (summer)
Location: UNM North Golf Course
Note: Near the UNM Law School on the east side of the golf course. Location may vary occasionally for other workout types.
Thursday
Track Workout

Track and Field workouts. Occasional time trials in place of regular workouts — published in member email in advance.

Time: 5:45 PM warmup & drills (summer), workout begins shortly after
Location: La Cueva High School track
Note: Location may vary occasionally if the track is in use.
Sunday
Long Run

Long run — typically 5 to 15 miles. Time and location vary by season and weather. Often on the Bosque Trail starting at the Alameda Open Space. Contact us for details.

Members receive a weekly email with full workout details, field drill suggestions, total body/cardio alternatives, and upcoming event announcements. Time trials are held periodically for progress checks and goal-setting.
