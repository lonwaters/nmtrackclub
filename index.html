About the Club

New Mexico Track Club is open to all adults — whether you're a sprinter, long-distance runner, thrower, jumper, or simply want to get fitter. Fitness comes first: core stability, nutrition, injury prevention, and healthy lifestyle habits anchor everything we do. Running and field events are built on top of that foundation.

Come check out a session before you commit. We'd love to meet you.

— Karen, Director
